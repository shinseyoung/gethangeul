Rules

Auto ads off Hand-placed only. Auto ads will inject into the quiz and undo every decision on this page.
Label outside the frame The word ADVERTISEMENT sits above the well, never inside it, and never says anything encouraging.
24px from any control No unit within 24px of a button. Accidental clicks are the fastest way to lose an account, and they are worth nothing anyway.
Reserve the height Every slot keeps its box before the ad arrives. Otherwise the page jumps as units fill and the layout-shift score drops.
Nothing on the card itself The name card is what gets screenshot and posted. An ad inside the capture frame turns free distribution into something nobody shares.
