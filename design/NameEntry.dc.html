하준

Hajun

夏俊
hah · joon

Hajun (하준) is a Korean given name written with the hanja 夏俊 — “summer” and “outstanding.” It reads as a person whose talent shows the way full summer shows: without effort, and impossible to miss. It is one of the most common boys' names registered in Korea over the last decade, and it works comfortably as a gender‑neutral choice.

What 하준 means

夏 하
Summer The season everything is already grown into. In names it carries heat, abundance and confidence rather than beginnings.
俊 준
Outstanding, talented One of the most-used characters in modern Korean names. It means a person who stands out — by ability, not by volume.

Together: a passionate soul, burning as brightly as the summer sun.

How to say it

Two beats, even stress — Koreans do not stress one syllable over the other. 하 is “hah,” the vowel in father, cut short. 준 is “joon,” close to the month June but shorter and with the tongue further forward.

The common mistake is stretching it to “HAH‑joooon.” Keep both halves the same length and you will sound right.
