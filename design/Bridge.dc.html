THE FIX IS CHEAP

Korean has plenty of given names starting with ㅂ — 보람 · 보윤 · 별 · 봄 · 빛나 — and none of them are in the file. Adding a dozen closes the B and P gap, which is roughly one English name in eight.

ㅋ and ㅍ are different: Korean given names almost never begin with them. That is a fact about the language, not a hole in the data. Route K to ㄱ and P/F to ㅂ, and say so on screen rather than pretending the match is exact.

AND DELETE ONE LINE

nameMatcher.ts adds Math.random() * 0.1 as a tiebreak. Same four answers, different name every reload. A friend who answers exactly as you did gets someone else's name — which is the one conversation that would have spread this site.

Take it out. Same name plus same answers gives the same result, forever. Retry becomes an explicit walk down the ranking — second, third — which is the Choose screen you already have, and it is better than a reroll because the visitor knows what they are trading.
